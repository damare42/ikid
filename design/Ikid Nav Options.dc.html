Navigation architecture

Where twelve sections should live.

The single top bar is full at nine links, and Accounts, Admin and Sign out still need a home. Three ways to carry the full app — each shows exactly where those three land. Compare 1a, 1b, 1c.

1a · Sidebar rail

All sections move to a grouped left rail, so the top is free for the page title and actions. Scales past twelve items without crowding. Identity sits at the bottom of the rail — Dawit → Settings · Admin · Sign out.
