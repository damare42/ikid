What it does

From raw statements to real decisions

📄

Import anything

Drop a CSV or PDF from any bank. Columns are detected automatically, duplicates are skipped, and 190+ rules categorise as they land.

🧠

Learns your spending

Correct a category once and ikid remembers it — and retroactively fixes past transactions from the same merchant.

📊

See where it goes

Dashboard, trends, category and merchant breakdowns, recurring-payment detection, and a conscious-spending view. Click any chart to see the transactions behind it.

🎯

Budgets & goals

Monthly limits with end-of-month forecasts, and savings goals that project their own completion date.

💎

Net worth

Track assets and liabilities over time. Loans show a projected payoff date and the interest remaining.

🧭

Plan retirement properly

Model FIRE, Coast FIRE, and early retirement across 401k, Roth, HSA and brokerage — with Roth conversion ladders, RMDs, and the bridge to 59½.

Why local-first

The safest place for your financial data is your own computer.

ikid has nothing to leak, by design.

  • No bank credentials. You export statements yourself — ikid never asks for logins and has no aggregator.
  • No cloud account. Everything lives in a SQLite file on your machine.
  • No third-party calls. No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no telemetry to us.
  • Optional AI stays local. Install Ollama and the planner answers freeform questions on your own hardware — or skip it and the deterministic engine still does the maths.
  • Open source. Read every line. The privacy claim is architectural, not a promise you have to trust.

Get started

Honest limitations

What ikid is not

Not automatic

There's no bank sync — you export statements yourself. That's the trade for never sharing credentials.

Not tax advice

The retirement and tax tools use real federal tables and are unit-tested, but they're planning support. Confirm decisions with a professional.

Not a hosted service

There's no ikid server holding your data. If you want access from anywhere, you host it yourself.
